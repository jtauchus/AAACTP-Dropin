How Friday Drop-In works

From adding your name to your next match — the whole board, in eight steps.

Use the arrows, the keyboard, or click either edge of the screen to move through it.
The big picture

Everything runs on one loop

Sign up once. After that, every match you play just cycles you back through the same four stages — waiting, pool, court, finish — until you head home.

Nobody manages this by hand. The board keeps the loop turning; you just drag things when you want to change how it turns.

Step 1 · before the board goes live

Add your name any time during the week

Tap + Sign up whenever — Tuesday night, Friday afternoon, it doesn't matter. You're just getting in line early.

Nothing happens with your name yet. It just sits on the list until the board goes live for the night.

Step 5 · game, set

Winners split up, losers rejoin the line

Tap Won for whichever side took it. The winning pair splits apart and waits for two new partners — so you don't play with the same person twice in a row. The losing pair heads back to the waiting line.

The board also remembers who's already partnered or played whom, and favors fresh match-ups when it forms new foursomes.

Bonus · when two courts finish together

Winners can play winners, losers can play losers

If another court finishes around the same time, a ⚡ Combine button shows up on both winner pools (or both loser pools) — one tap merges them straight into a new match instead of waiting for the general line.

Nobody's forced into it — if no match is available to combine with, your pool just tops up from the waiting list like normal.

Step 6 · when numbers get odd

Odd headcount? Courts go into Server-Out

Every even number off-court can split cleanly into 2s and 4s. An odd number always leaves one person short — so instead of someone sitting out, every court switches to Server-Out.

Each on-court player gets their own Out button: tap it and the longest-waiting person swaps straight in for you.

Step 7 · heading out or grabbing water

Tap your name for your options

On a court and need a breather? Move to waiting pulls you off without losing your spot in the rotation.

Done for the night? Remove from play checks you out entirely. Either way, the court shows an open slot for someone to fill.

Step 8 · from your own phone

The board's just a link away

Two QR codes live in the header: one drops you straight into the club's WhatsApp group, the other opens this exact live board on your own phone — no app, no login.

Check in, watch the waiting list, or drag your own name into a pool, right from your pocket.
